# Your account

Use an account on the min.gd instance you are visiting to create builds, save
recipes and access your private history. A recipe share link can be viewed
without an account; using it to build or save a copy requires signing in.

## Create an account and sign in

Choose **Sign in → Create account**, enter an email and password, and submit.
New passwords must be 8–128 characters. If the app asks you to confirm your
email, follow the confirmation email and then sign in. **Resend confirmation**
is available on the sign-in form.

After signing in, the Builds menu contains **All builds**, **New build** and
**Saved recipes**. Open the account menu for **Account settings** or **Sign out**.
Signing out here ends the current session.

## Profile, email and password

In Account settings:

- Edit **Display name** and choose **Save profile**.
- Use **Use my Gravatar** to enable or disable the avatar associated with your
  email. Change the picture on Gravatar; min.gd has no avatar-upload control.
- Enter a new email and choose **Change email**. Follow the confirmation
  instructions sent by the app's authentication service.
- To change your password while signed in, enter the current password, the new
  password and its confirmation, then choose **Update password**.

## Forgotten password

Choose **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page and enter your account email.
Open the reset email, enter and confirm a new password, then use it to sign in.
If the link has expired or was already used, request a fresh email.

If emails never arrive or links open the wrong site, see [help](user-help.md).
The instance operator controls email delivery and authentication configuration.

## Disabled accounts

An administrator can disable an account. The app then blocks protected pages,
new submissions and new downloads. This keeps the account and build records;
it does not cancel builds already queued. Contact your instance administrator
if you need access restored.

## Site announcements

Signed-in users may see messages below the navigation. You can collapse them,
dismiss individual messages or all of them, and reopen the announcement bar.
Preferences are kept in that browser, not synchronized across devices. New or
edited messages appear again.
